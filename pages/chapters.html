Arc I · 845 C.E.

The Fall of Shiganshina

Vol. 1–2 · Ch. 1–12

The story begins. The Colossal Titan appears outside Wall Maria — a Titan so vast its face peers over the top of the wall. It kicks a breach into the gate, and Shiganshina District falls in hours. Eren Jaeger watches his mother Carla devoured as Hannes carries him to safety. Humanity loses one-third of its territory in a single day. The 104th Training Corps is born from the ashes.

Arc II · 847 C.E.

The 104th Training Corps

Vol. 3–4 · Ch. 13–22

Three years after the fall. Eren, Mikasa, and Armin train alongside 218 other cadets under Commander Keith Shadis. Bonds are forged: the cold genius of Jean Kirstein, the devout faith of Krista Lenz, the stoic strength of Reiner Braun, the sharp instincts of Annie Leonhart. The class graduates, and the top ten enlist in the Survey Corps — the branch that fights Titans beyond the Walls.

  • Graduation: top 10 choose the Survey Corps
  • Eren's desperation — he must join the Survey Corps to fight Titans directly
Arc III · 847 C.E.

The Battle of Trost

Vol. 4–6 · Ch. 23–35

The Colossal Titan appears again — this time at Trost District, the gateway to Wall Rose. The Armored Titan breaches the gate. Hundreds are trapped inside, including the newly graduated cadets. In the chaos, Eren is devoured. But he emerges moments later as the Attack Titan — humanity's first weapon against the Titans. He lifts a massive boulder to seal the breach. The world changes forever.

Arc IV · 848 C.E.

The 57th Expedition — Female Titan

Vol. 7–10 · Ch. 36–50

The Survey Corps launches the 57th Expedition beyond the Walls — a bait operation to capture the Titan that has been killing their squads. A mysterious Female Titan appears, capable of selectively hardening her skin and summoning Pure Titans with her scream. She pursues Eren relentlessly. The operation becomes a slaughter. Only Levi's intervention prevents total annihilation.

Arc V · 849 C.E.

Clash of the Titans

Vol. 10–13 · Ch. 51–72

The Survey Corps fortifies Wall Rose, knowing the Titans are inside. But nothing prepares them for the truth. In a moment of fragile stillness atop a wall, Reiner Braun turns to Eren and confesses: "I am the Armored Titan. He is the Colossal." Bertholdt Hoover stands beside him. Identity shatters. War begins within humanity. The battle that follows — in the trees, on the walls, in the sky — costs everything.

Arc VI · 849 C.E.

The Uprising

Vol. 13–18 · Ch. 73–108

The story pivots. The true enemy is not beyond the Walls — it is within. The Interior Police under First King Kenney Ackerman move against the Survey Corps. The monarchy's secrets unravel. Historia Reiss is revealed as the true heir to the throne. In the caverns beneath the Reiss chapel, Eren and Historia confront her father — and the truth of the Founding Titan's power. The Walls were never a prison. They were a tomb.

Arc VII · 850 C.E.

Return to Shiganshina

Vol. 18–22 · Ch. 109–138

The Survey Corps launches its most audacious mission — retake Wall Maria and reach the basement of Eren's old home. The answer to everything lies there. But Reiner and Bertholdt are waiting. The battle for Shiganshina is a slaughter. Erwin leads the final cavalry charge into the Beast Titan's rain of debris. Levi makes the choice. The basement reveals the truth: beyond the Walls, humanity is not extinct. The enemy is the world.

The Marley Arc — Declaration of War

Arc IX · 854 C.E.

War for Paradis · The Rumbling

Vol. 28–34 · Ch. 179–238

The final act. Eren unleashes the Rumbling — the Colossal Titans sealed within the Walls march across the ocean. The world unites against Paradis in a desperate last stand. Former enemies become allies. Mikasa, Armin, Levi, and the surviving scouts pursue Eren through Paths, through the Founding Titan's colossal form, and to the final moment that determines the fate of all Titankind. The story that began at the fall of Shiganshina ends with a choice made 2,000 years in the making.
