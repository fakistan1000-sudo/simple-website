Lore Timeline

2,000 Years of History — The Complete Chronology

~2,000 Years Ago

The Birth of the Titans — Ymir Fritz

A young slave girl named Ymir stumbles upon a mysterious tree and gains the power of the Titans — becoming the first Titan Shifter. She serves King Fritz, bearing his children and building the Eldian Empire through the Titans' overwhelming might. Upon her death, her soul is split into Nine, and her bloodline becomes the Subjects of Ymir — bound for eternity through the Paths.

The Founding Titan's Reign — The Eldian Empire

For nearly 1,700 years, the Founding Titan passes through the royal bloodline. The Eldian Empire conquers the world using the power of the Titans. Marley and other nations are subjugated, and the Subjects of Ymir spread across the continents. The Nine Titan families rule with absolute authority.

~100 Years Ago

The Great Titan War

The Eldian Empire crumbles from within. A devastating civil war erupts among the Nine Titan families. Marley, once a subjugated nation, rises against Eldia. The rulers of the Titans turn against each other, and the empire that dominated the world for two millennia falls into chaos. The Blood Lake of the Titan battlefield becomes legend.

King Karl Fritz — The Walls Are Built

King Karl Fritz, the 145th inheritor of the Founding Titan, abandons the mainland conflict. He leads a mass exodus of Eldians to Paradis Island — a remote land across the ocean. Using the power of the Founding Titan, he commands the construction of three colossal Walls: Maria, Rose, and Sina. The Walls are built from hardened Colossal Titans in a state of suspended animation. He alters the memories of his people, making them believe humanity is extinct beyond the Walls. A paradise built on a lie.

The Tybur Family — The Vow of Peace

The Tybur family, inheritors of the War Hammer Titan, collaborate with Marley. They orchestrate the narrative: King Fritz took the Founding Titan to Paradis and will not act as long as peace is maintained. Marley distributes the remaining Seven Titans among its Warrior candidates. The world settles into an uneasy equilibrium — Paradis isolated, Marley ascendant, the truth buried.

845 C.E.

The Fall of Shiganshina

The Colossal Titan appears outside Wall Maria — a Titan so vast its face peers over the top of the wall. It kicks a breach into the gate, and the Armored Titan follows. Shiganshina District falls in hours. Eren Jaeger watches his mother Carla devoured as Hannes carries him and Mikasa to safety. Humanity loses one-third of its territory in a single day. The reign of terror inside the Walls begins.

847–848 C.E.

The 104th Training Corps

Eren, Mikasa, Armin, and 218 other cadets enlist under Commander Keith Shadis. For three years they train in ODM gear, hand-to-hand combat, and Titan survival tactics. Bonds form: Jean, Connie, Sasha, Historia, Ymir — and the Warriors: Reiner, Bertholdt, Annie. The top ten graduates choose the Survey Corps.

The Battle of Trost — Eren Awakens

The Colossal Titan breaches Wall Rose at Trost. Trapped inside, Eren is devoured by a Pure Titan — but emerges moments later as the Attack Titan. He lifts a boulder to seal the breach, saving the district. The Survey Corps takes him into custody. For the first time, humanity has a weapon against the Titans. The Assembly debates his fate.

848–849 C.E.

The 57th Expedition — The Female Titan

The Survey Corps launches a bait operation to lure out a mysterious enemy. The Female Titan decimates Levi's squad. Eren is captured. The Corps fights back — capturing the Female Titan in the forest. She is revealed as Annie Leonhart. She crystallizes herself to avoid interrogation. The first crack in the facade appears. Not all Titans are mindless monsters.

The Clash of the Titans — Reiner's Confession

On top of Wall Rose, Reiner Braun breaks. He confesses: "I am the Armored Titan. Bertholdt is the Colossal." The battle that follows tears through a forest, a tower, and the walls themselves. Erwin devises a plan to capture them. Ymir reveals her Jaw Titan powers. Bertholdt and Reiner escape with Ymir and Eren captured. The truth is out — the enemy is human.

849–850 C.E.

The Uprising — Truth of the Walls

The Survey Corps is framed for treason. The Interior Police under Kenney Ackerman hunt them. Erwin is arrested, then freed in a coup. The Corps discovers the Reiss family's true nature as the royal bloodline. In the chapel beneath the Reiss estate, Eren confronts Historia's father. Historia chooses to live for herself. Eren consumes the Founding Titan's power. The Walls' secret is revealed.

Return to Shiganshina — The Basement

The Survey Corps launches its most desperate mission. Erwin leads a cavalry charge against the Beast Titan's barrage. Levi makes the impossible choice: Erwin or Armin. Armin inherits the Colossal Titan. The basement holds Grisha Jaeger's books — revealing that humanity exists beyond the Walls. The world is not empty. It is hostile. The enemy is not Titans. It is everyone else.

854 C.E.

The Ocean — Three Years Later

Eren, Mikasa, and Armin stand at the edge of the sea — the place they dreamed of since childhood. But Eren points across the water and asks: "If we kill all our enemies on the other side... will we finally be free?" The question hangs in the salt air. The Survey Corps has become a military power. Marley prepares for war. Paradis prepares to strike first.

Declaration of War — Liberio Falls

Eren infiltrates Marley undercover. In the Liberio theater, Willy Tybur declares war on Paradis. Eren transforms mid-speech, killing Willy and the War Hammer Titan's inheritor. The Survey Corps attacks from the sky. Zeke defects to Paradis. The world's war becomes Paradis's war. There is no going back.

The Rumbling — The World Trembles

Eren activates the Founding Titan's full power. Millions of Colossal Titans sealed within the Walls rise and march into the ocean. The Rumbling begins — the annihilation of the world beyond Paradis. Former enemies unite against a common threat. The alliance of Marleyan Warriors and Survey Corps soldiers pursues Eren through the Paths, through the Founding Titan, and to the final confrontation that will decide the fate of Titankind.

The Final Stand — The Curse Ends

In the Paths, Zeke and Armin confront Ymir Fritz herself — the slave girl who started it all 2,000 years ago. Mikasa makes the choice that frees Ymir from her bondage to love and duty. The Founding Titan's power dissolves. The Colossal Titans crumble into the sea. The curse of the Titans ends. Eren dies, giving his friends a world without Titans. The story that began with "To you, 2,000 years from now" ends with a tree, a hill, and a boy who walks toward it.

Attack on Titan (進撃の巨人) by Hajime Isayama · Kodansha · 2009–2021
