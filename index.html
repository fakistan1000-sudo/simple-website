Encyclopedia Titanica

Titan Registry

The Attack Titan

進撃の巨人

A relentless Titan that moves forward through every age, always fighting for freedom. Its unique ability allows it to see the memories of its future inheritors. Currently held by Eren Jaeger.

The Colossal Titan

超大型巨人

Towering at 60 meters, this Titan's transformation unleashes a devastating explosion of steam and light. Its immense size makes it the ultimate siege weapon. Currently held by Armin Arlert.

The Armored Titan

鎧の巨人

Covered in near-impenetrable armor plating, this Titan is a living battering ram. It breached Wall Maria and Wall Rose. Its armored fists can shatter anything. Held by Reiner Braun.

The Female Titan

女型の巨人

A highly agile and versatile Titan capable of hardening its skin into crystalline armor. Its scream can summon nearby Pure Titans. Intelligent and deadly in combat. Held by Annie Leonhart.

The Beast Titan

獣の巨人

An ape-like Titan with extraordinary intelligence. Its long arms allow it to hurl projectiles with devastating accuracy. Can control Pure Titans through its roar. Held by Zeke Jaeger.

The Jaw Titan

顎の巨人

Small, swift, and ferocious. Its razor-sharp teeth and claws can tear through even the hardest materials, including Titan armor. Unmatched in close-quarters combat. Agile and unpredictable.

The Cart Titan

車力の巨人

A quadrupedal Titan built for endurance over burst combat. The Cart Titan can maintain its form for extended periods — days or even weeks — making it ideal for reconnaissance, artillery transport, and sustained operations. Its back serves as a mobile weapons platform.

The War Hammer Titan

戦槌の巨人

One of the rarest and most lethal of the Nine. The War Hammer Titan can conjure weapons from hardened Titan flesh — spears, crossbows, blades, and a colossal war hammer. Its user controls it from a distance via a crystalized tether, making it nearly impossible to defeat in close combat.

The Founding Titan

始祖の巨人

The first of all Titans — the Founding Titan commands the Coordinate that binds every Subject of Ymir across time and space. It can control all Titans, alter the memories and biology of Eldians, and unleash the Rumbling. The power that built the Walls can also tear the world apart.

Chronicles

Lore Timeline

~2000 Years Ago
Ymir Fritz — The First Titan
A young slave girl named Ymir stumbles upon a mysterious tree and gains the power of the Titans. She becomes the Founding Titan, serving King Fritz and building the Eldian Empire. Upon her death, her soul splits into Nine, and her bloodline becomes the Subjects of Ymir — bound across time through the Paths.
~100 Years Ago
The Great Titan War · Founding of the Walls
The Eldian Empire crumbles in a devastating civil war. King Karl Fritz, inheritor of the Founding Titan, abandons the mainland and leads his people to Paradis Island. He commands the construction of three colossal Walls — Maria, Rose, and Sina — built from hardened Titans. The world believes Eldia is extinct.
845 C.E.
The Fall of Wall Maria
The Colossal Titan appears outside Wall Maria — a Titan so vast its face peers over the top. It kicks a breach into the gate. Shiganshina District falls in hours. Eren Jaeger watches his mother Carla devoured as Hannes carries him to safety. Humanity loses one-third of its territory in a single day.
847 C.E.
The 104th Training Corps
Eren, Mikasa, and Armin enlist in the 104th Training Corps alongside 218 other cadets. Under Commander Keith Shadis, they undergo brutal training in ODM gear, hand-to-hand combat, and Titan tactics. Bonds are forged that will be tested to their breaking point beyond the Walls.
850 C.E.
The Battle of Trost — Eren Awakens
The Colossal and Armored Titans breach Wall Rose at Trost District. Trapped inside, Eren is devoured by a Pure Titan — only to emerge moments later as the Attack Titan. He lifts a boulder to seal the breach. Humanity strikes back for the first time. The Survey Corps takes Eren into their ranks.
850 C.E.
The Truth — Reiner and Bertholdt
During the 57th Expedition, the Female Titan is captured — revealed to be Annie Leonhart. In the Clash of the Titans arc, Reiner Braun breaks. He confesses: he is the Armored Titan. Bertholdt Hoover is the Colossal. They were sent from beyond the Walls. The world as the scouts knew it shatters forever.
854 C.E.
The Rumbling · The Final Stand
Eren activates the Founding Titan and unleashes the Rumbling — millions of Colossal Titans sealed within the Walls march across the ocean. The world unites against Paradis. In a final battle that spans Heaven and Earth, the surviving scouts fight to stop Eren. The Titan Curse ends. Freedom comes at an unimaginable cost.
